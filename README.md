## Projects

### [Distributed ClickHouse Analytics Warehouse Modernization](https://sa01.github.io/resume/#project-clickhouse-warehouse-modernization)
Led the architecture, benchmarks and proof of concept to replace MongoDB with a sharded ClickHouse warehouse for multi-tenant cost analytics, scaling processing capacity by 50% to over 1 billion rows per run. **79% lower median query latency**, and six-month data loads cut from five days to one. Run with Claude Code, Skills and hooks for reproducible, evidence-backed decisions.

`ClickHouse` `PySpark` `Ray` `Kubernetes` `DuckDB` `Claude Code`

### [Multi-cloud Cost Processing and Attribution](https://sa01.github.io/resume/#project-multi-cloud-cost-processing)
Architected the AWS cost pipelines that became the core of the platform, then extended them to Azure, Google Cloud, Kubernetes container costs and rule-based business hierarchies, at **up to 2.5 billion rows per run**. Fixed data skew, applied bucketing and partitioning, and used spot instances to cut runtime **30%** and cost **15%**.

`Apache Spark` `Scala` `Amazon EMR` `Kubernetes` `Airflow` `Terraform`

### Anomaly Detection and Forecasting
[Anomaly detection](https://sa01.github.io/resume/#project-anomaly-detection) over 6–8 billion rows of cost data using statistical analysis, functional approximation and outlier detection, catching **$600K** of runaway spend for Zoom. [Forecasting](https://sa01.github.io/resume/#project-budgeting-and-forecasting): an ensemble of three models producing **24-month forecasts** at every level of the business hierarchy, tuned with cross-validation and back-testing on RMSE and MAPE, delivered in under 3 months.

`Python` `Apache Spark` `facebook-prophet` `Kubeflow`

### [Reserved Instance Recommendations](https://sa01.github.io/resume/#project-reserved-instance-recommendations)
Simulated AWS's Reserved Instance application algorithm on hourly EC2 inventory and optimized convertible RI exchanges with a mixed-integer linear program, plus near-real-time cost estimates that bridge the 2–3 day billing lag. **A key capability in closing Intuit as a customer.**

`Python` `PuLP` `pandas` `Amazon EC2`
